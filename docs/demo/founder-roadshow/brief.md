# NodeSlide AI Fund roadshow brief

Create a six-slide AI Fund roadshow deck for NodeSlide.

Audience: early-stage investors who understand AI products but do not need implementation-level detail.

Goal: explain the presentation-workflow problem, NodeSlide's structured-authoring insight, how evidence becomes editable visuals, measured prototype proof, the initial founder/researcher wedge, and the next validation milestone.

Use these sources:

- Product: https://parity-studio.vercel.app/
- Research: https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.00912
- Uploaded product requirements and technical design documents
- Uploaded prototype-metrics.csv
- Uploaded customer-notes.md
- Uploaded design-reference.md

Requirements:

- Distinguish measured prototype evidence from future targets.
- Cite every external factual claim.
- Turn the system workflow into an editable diagram.
- Turn prototype metrics into a native editable chart with units and source binding.
- Include one editable math primitive that explains the north-star metric.
- Use an image only when its source and reuse rights can be stated honestly.
- Add plain-language speaker notes.
- Keep the deck to six slides and make every slide do one clear job.

Suggested argument:

1. Founders do not lack information; they lack a trustworthy way to turn scattered evidence into a story.
2. NodeSlide first creates an evidence model and a story model, then compiles editable slides.
3. Web, files, and prior material become diagrams, charts, sourced claims, and speaker notes.
4. The prototype proves controlled revision: proposals, human acceptance, validation, lineage, and editable export.
5. The wedge is recurring, evidence-heavy founder and researcher decks.
6. The next milestone is a measured design-partner pilot, not a fabricated traction claim.
