# NodeSlide super-foraging operating model

Date: 2026-07-11
Status: product-research contract; not an autonomous web-ingestion capability

## Definition

Super-foraging is NodeSlide's evidence-led process for exploring products, research, open-source systems, datasets, and domain workflows; extracting transferable mechanisms with provenance; synthesizing an original product variation; and proving that variation against owned or licensed artifacts.

It is not permission to copy branding, layouts, proprietary prompts, private data, or undocumented internals. Public observation supports hypotheses, not claims of competitor implementation knowledge.

## Operating loop

1. **Explore** — collect primary evidence about a bounded product or workflow question.
2. **Extract** — describe the reusable mechanism, user expectation, constraint, and failure mode rather than its surface styling.
3. **Relate** — record why the mechanism worked, for whom, and under what evidence.
4. **Synthesize** — combine compatible mechanisms into an original NodeSlide hypothesis.
5. **Differentiate** — state the new authority model, workflow, vertical adaptation, or proof boundary.
6. **Build** — implement the smallest reversible variation behind independent controls.
7. **Prove** — run deterministic checks, comparative evaluation, native-artifact review, and human critique.
8. **Record** — retain source links, dates, license tier, decisions, rejected alternatives, digests, and launch verdict.

## Required records

Every extracted pattern must carry:

- canonical source URL, publisher/owner, observation date, and evidence type;
- the exact claim supported by that source and the limits of the observation;
- license/rights tier and whether material may be embedded, referenced, or must remain quarantined;
- the transferable mechanism and the constraints that make it work;
- NodeSlide's differentiated hypothesis and the subsystem it changes;
- a test case, success/failure metric, owner, and re-check trigger;
- a prohibition on importing credentials, customer data, proprietary artifacts, or unlicensed media.

## IP and claim boundary

NodeSlide owns the code, contracts, fixtures, authored dogfood decks, taxonomies, and evaluation receipts created in this repository. External facts and mechanisms remain attributed to their sources. Competitor names are used only for factual comparison; no competitor assets, private behavior, or inferred proprietary architecture become NodeSlide fixtures.

Marketing may claim only what frozen receipts demonstrate: editable source-aware exports, bounded reviewable execution infrastructure, and controlled-preview safety behavior. It may not claim autonomous professional-quality authoring, benchmark leadership, or parity with a competitor from public observation alone.

## Stop conditions

Stop extraction or launch promotion when provenance is missing, rights are ambiguous, a claim depends on inferred internals, the variation cannot be independently tested, safety and quality are collapsed into one score, or a public/multi-tenant prerequisite is being treated as a documentation waiver.
