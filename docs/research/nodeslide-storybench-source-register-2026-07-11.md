# NodeSlide StoryBench source and license register

Date checked: 2026-07-11
Default posture: no benchmark material enters NodeSlide fixtures unless its exact files, license, provenance, and permitted use are verified
Implementation gate: `convex/lib/nodeslideStoryBench.ts`

## Tier definitions

| Tier | Meaning | StoryBench treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A — usable | Exact benchmark artifacts have explicit licenses compatible with the planned local evaluation use, and required attribution/provenance can be preserved. | May be pinned, hashed, and used as embedded fixtures after a file-level intake manifest is reviewed. |
| B — evaluate by reference | The paper, code, or metadata is public, but the dataset contains third-party material, has a restrictive license, or lacks file-level redistribution clarity. | Keep URLs and revisions only. Obtain material from the official source at evaluation time where permitted; do not redistribute it in this repository or derived launch artifacts. |
| C — quarantined | Dataset/release is unavailable, incomplete, ambiguously licensed, or prohibited for the intended use. | Do not download into the evaluation environment, score for promotion, train on, redistribute, or derive fixtures from it. Re-review only when the owner publishes a clear release/license. |

Code licenses do not automatically license benchmark inputs, generated product outputs, papers, videos, or third-party background documents.

## Intake checklist for Tier A material

Before a file can become an embedded StoryBench fixture, record:

1. canonical source URL and retrieval date;
2. upstream creator and title;
3. exact license or rights statement and required attribution;
4. SHA-256 of the original download;
5. whether modification, commercial evaluation, redistribution, and publication of screenshots/outputs are allowed;
6. whether fonts, images, videos, logos, charts, and linked assets have separate rights;
7. the minimal local derivative, if any, and its derivation recipe;
8. a case ID, fixture digest, permitted material mode, and deletion/re-review owner;
9. malware and active-content inspection for Office files;
10. written approval before any benchmark output is used in public marketing.

## StoryBench launch policy

- Private-preview CI currently uses NodeSlide-authored deterministic fixtures. A small PPT-Eval subset may be added only after exact-file hash, attribution, active-content, and license manifests are reviewed.
- Tier B cases may produce internal comparison reports, but source artifacts are never committed and results must identify the official revision and access method.
- Tier C material is blocked by the StoryBench promotion gate.
- No benchmark result becomes a marketing claim without a frozen harness version, adapter version, case manifest, raw receipts, cost/latency accounting, and a reproducible rerun.
- LLM-judge scores are supporting evidence, not sole launch gates. Deterministic structure, lineage, version safety, editability, and render checks remain mandatory.
