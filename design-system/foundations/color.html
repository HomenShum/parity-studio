Color — instrument and ink

The chrome frames other people's brands, so it stays a quiet instrument. Color carries exactly two messages: who touched this (authorship inks) and can you trust it (receipt semantics). Derived from NodeRoom; nothing here is decorative.

Terracotta is the human's pen. Indigo is the agent's pen. Every patch, selection outline, proposal card, and version entry is signed with its author's ink. Agents never write in terracotta — an agent action can never impersonate a human one.
